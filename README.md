## About

I teach Informatics at University of Mataram and build web-based information systems, mostly with PHP and Laravel. Much of my work is developing systems for schools, campuses, and communities, and supervising students' final projects.

## Research Focus

My current research is on artificial intelligence applied to medical imaging, using machine learning and deep learning to analyze images in the healthcare domain.

- Medical image analysis and classification
- Deep learning for healthcare imaging
- Applying AI results in practical, web-based systems
